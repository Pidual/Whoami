I studied Systems and Computer Engineering at UPTC in Tunja and fell into governance, risk and compliance during my internship at Banco de Bogotá, where the ISO 27001 audit process ran on spreadsheets and email chains. So I built a replacement. That project became my thesis and got a nod from the external auditor.

Now I work remotely for a US company, keeping SOC 2 and ISO 27001 controls on track, writing policies, and answering customer security questionnaires with Engineering, Legal and Product. Outside work I run a Proxmox homelab, train image models, rice my setup, play way too much Deadlock, and occasionally win hackathons.

Based in Yopal, Casanare. Remote is my favorite; hybrid or on-site anywhere in Colombia works too.
